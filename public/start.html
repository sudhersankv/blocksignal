BlockSignal

BlockSignal shows where people in San Francisco are missing help, block by block.

Open the live map → How it works

The problemReports count complaints, not need

  1. San Francisco's 311 line gets thousands of encampment reports a month.
  2. Those reports show where people complain. They don't show where people lack help.
  3. Help, like Pit Stop restrooms, is often far away or closed, especially at night.
  4. So outreach vans and city resources follow the loudest complaints, not the biggest gaps.

At 9 PM tonight, only — of — Pit Stops are open.

Live numbers could not load right now. In a typical 30 days, 311 gets about 3,000 to 4,000 encampment reports, and most Pit Stops are closed at 9 PM.

Problem statementThe gap nobody can see

Outreach teams and the Mayor's Office can't easily see which blocks have many reports and no open help nearby, right now.

How we fix itThree steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Combine

    Live 311 encampment reports + Pit Stop locations and opening hours.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Score

    Every block gets a Gap score.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Act

    Ask Outreach Compass, our AI analyst, a plain question. It answers with ranked blocks, van routes and memos, and it shows its numbers.

Key terms

Block
A map square about 330 m wide (2–3 city blocks).
Gap score
0–100: how underserved a block is right now. Higher = more reports, more recent, and farther from open help. 100 = the most underserved block in the city today.
Open help
A Pit Stop that is open at the time you picked.
Service gap
Many reports + no open help nearby.

Why it's credibleBuilt with Mistral and Elastic

Real city data, real search, and an AI analyst that shows its numbers.

Mistral

  • Reads messy Pit Stop hours and crew notes into clean data (structured outputs)
  • Runs the Outreach Compass analyst (function calling)
  • Searches notes by meaning (mistral-embed)

Elastic

  • Stores every report on the map (geo_point)
  • Finds where reports cluster (geotile aggregations)
  • Measures distance to open help (geo_distance)
  • Searches notes by meaning (semantic_text)
  • Tracks weekly trends (ES|QL)
  • Runs the same analyst in Kibana (Agent Builder, shareable over MCP)
