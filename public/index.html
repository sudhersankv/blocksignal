      What is the gap score?

      Gap score (0–100): how underserved a block is right now. Higher = more reports, more recent, and farther from open help. 100 = the most underserved block in the city today.

      It goes up with more 311 encampment reports in the last 30 days, more of them in the last week, and more distance to the nearest Pit Stop that is open at the time you picked. It's relative, not an absolute count.
